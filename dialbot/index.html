Meeting etiquette · North Star

The room keeps
moving.

Looking up a Jira ticket can take time. Dial Bot acknowledges the ask, gives a natural update if the search runs long, and lets everyone keep talking. When it is ready, it waits for a pause and an invitation to answer.

Dial Bot

“I’m still checking the linked items. This may take a moment.”

Engineer

“I can start on the failed-join fix while you look.”

Dial Bot

“I have the list whenever you’re ready.”

PM

“Go ahead.”

This is conversational timing, not a promise of instant AI answers. The waiting and invitation flow is a future-state design.

Context, by invitation · North Star

The right context.
Only when shared.

People connect their own Glean access and choose what the meeting may use. Dial Bot can draw on that shared context to answer a question aloud, point back to a source, or propose a follow-up.

Before a consequential action—such as reassigning a Jira ticket—it reads back the exact change and asks the right person to confirm.

Why build it

The meeting and the agent
can move together.

Dialpad can shape how Bot joins the room, listens, waits, speaks, and leaves the work with its owners. That is the experience this concept explores.
