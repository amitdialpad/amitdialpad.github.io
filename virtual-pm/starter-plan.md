# Our shared plan

> Replace these prompts with your own words. Keep this plan where your team can find it. The person doing a piece of work, or their AI agent, updates the relevant section when that work changes.

## What we're making

**Goal:** What are we trying to make, and for whom?

**Why it matters:** What problem or opportunity brought us here?

**What a good first result looks like:** What can we show or test?

## Where we are now

Write a short, honest snapshot. Separate what has been shown to work from what is only an idea or a mockup.

## Keep this plan moving

1. Read this plan before starting work.
2. When your work changes, update your own row and any affected decision, blocker, or proof in the same contribution.
3. Ask an AI agent to check this plan against the work when useful. It only runs when you invoke it.
4. A person resolves product decisions and checks claims before the plan is shared as current.

This file is a starter, not an automatic monitor. It stays useful because the team keeps it current.
