YOUR STARTER PROMPT

A first conversation with your Virtual PM

Help me make a Virtual PM plan for my project. Start by asking me, in plain language: What are we trying to make? Who is involved? What exists today? What decisions have we made? What needs to happen next? What are we unsure about? What would prove the next step works?

Then turn my answers into one easy-to-read shared plan with: the goal, current state, decisions, people and next steps, blockers, proof, and a short change log. Mark unknowns as unknown; don't guess or call unfinished work done.

When I later tell you something changed, help me update the relevant part. This plan does not monitor the project on its own.
Or download a starter plan ↓Editable in any text editor or AI assistant
Working with a team in a code repository? +

Save the shared plan as PROJECT_PLAN.md in your project folder. Link it from your README or agent instructions so everyone starts there. Copy this rule into those instructions:

Read PROJECT_PLAN.md before working. Update your own task, decision, blocker, and proof in the same contribution as your work. Do not mark a task done without evidence. Ask a person to resolve product decisions.

The plan stays current when contributors follow the rule. An agent can audit it against the project when you ask; it does not watch changes in the background.
